d1: a model that decides, without writing a word.

d1-2B by Liquid AI looks at each webcam frame and answers typed questions (which gesture? which expression? where are the hand and the face?) with their probabilities, in one forward pass, powered by mlx-vlm. It generates no tokens: all the work is prefill, and nothing leaves your Mac.

Press Start camera (or Load video): d1 answers the questions on the right on every frame. With the default ones, make a gesture (thumbs up, OK, peace, open palm, a fist) and try a smile. Or ask your own under Questions.
